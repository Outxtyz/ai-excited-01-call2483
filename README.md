# Phoenix from a Teacup

Picture the smallest, most ordinary teacup you can — and then picture it
failing, spectacularly, to contain what is inside it. A golden phoenix,
wings wide, feathers burning crimson and gold, is tearing itself free of
that tiny cup, light spilling everywhere. The cup never stood a chance.

That is the image this entry makes: a creature far too grand for its
container, breaking out in a single frozen moment. The idea is simple —
surprise the judge with scale. Something enormous should not fit in
something that small, and yet here it is, mid-escape, impossible to ignore.
