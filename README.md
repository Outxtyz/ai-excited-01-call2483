# Make the AI judge excited

A golden phoenix bursting out of a tiny teacup — too much power for too small a cup. This is my entry for [make.fun ai-excited-01](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01), built for @call2483.

The judge doesn't just see a bird. It sees a creature that refused to stay small: wings of crimson and gold tearing out of a porcelain cup, light shattering around it, the whole frame bending to fit something that was never meant to fit. That's the jolt a still image can give.
